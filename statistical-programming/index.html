End-to-end statistical programming capabilities

Every clinical study demands complete traceability from raw data capture to regulatory filing. Our programming teams handle the entire biometrics scope under strict SOPs.

01

Tables, Listings and Figures (TLFs)

Production and validation of clear, publication- and submission-grade safety and efficacy tables, patient data listings, survival curves, forest plots, and shift tables for interim cuts, DSMBs/DMCs, DSURs, and final Clinical Study Reports (CSRs).

02

CDISC SDTM & ADaM Datasets

Standards-compliant specification writing, raw-to-SDTM mapping, and analysis-ready ADaM dataset derivation (ADSL, ADAE, ADTTE, ADLB, ADQS) accompanied by Annotated CRFs, Define-XML 2.0/2.1, and Study/Analysis Data Reviewer’s Guides.

03

ISS & ISE Data Pooling

Harmonisation and integration of multi-trial clinical databases (including legacy non-CDISC studies) for Integrated Summary of Safety (ISS) and Integrated Summary of Efficacy (ISE) regulatory modules.

04

PK/PD & NONMEM Programming

Population PK/PD input files, WinNonLin datasets, and submission-compliant ADPPK domains with covariate summaries and individual patient anomaly plots.

05

SAS Automation & Metalytics

Metadata-driven reporting through our in-house Metalytics platform and validated SAS macro repositories—eliminating black-box code while accelerating database lock turnaround.

Rigorous validation and independent QC workflow

Submission readiness is built into every step rather than bolted on at database lock. Our programmers adhere to strict Good Programming Practice (GPP) and double-programming verification.

  1. SAP & Shell Review

    Early programmer review of the Statistical Analysis Plan, mock TLF shells, and edit-check rules to catch ambiguities upfront.

  2. Specification & SDTM

    Machine-readable mapping specifications and raw EDC/lab transformation into CDISC SDTM domains with data issue tracking.

  3. ADaM Derivation

    Traceable analysis datasets implementing complex censoring, imputation, baseline flags, and therapeutic endpoint windows.

  4. Automated TLF Build

    Consistent generation of in-text and post-text tables, listings, and SGPLOT figures driven by validated macro templates.

  5. Double QC & Pinnacle 21

    100% independent parallel programming, automated SAS log scanning, and Pinnacle 21 validation for zero-discrepancy sign-off.
