About Shafi Consultancy

Experienced People. Trusted Programming. Quality You Can Rely On.

At Shafi Consultancy Limited, we help pharmaceutical companies, Contract Research Organisations (CROs), and biotechnology companies turn complex clinical trial data into reliable, submission-ready outputs. From clinical statistical programming, CDISC dataset development and Tables, Listings and Figures, to independent validation and PK/PD programming, we provide specialist support that helps clinical development teams work with greater confidence.

In clinical research, data quality matters: errors, inconsistencies, and delays in preparing clinical datasets and reports can create costly rework and put pressure on regulatory submission timelines. Our role is to help reduce these risks through careful programming, independent quality checks, and a clear understanding of the data behind every deliverable.

Our Story: Built on Experience

Shafi Consultancy was founded in the UK in 2001 by Shafi Chowdhury, drawing on his experience working with leading pharmaceutical companies and CROs. In 2006, we established our Bangladesh operation, building a dedicated team and expanding our ability to support clients internationally with the same team year in year out.

Over the years, we have grown our expertise in clinical statistical programming, CDISC standards, PK/PD datasets, NONMEM preparation, and independent programming validation. Our history reflects a commitment to technical excellence, long-term client relationships, and the people behind the work.

Today, we combine the experience of senior statistical programmers with a focused, collaborative team and purpose-built web applications that help streamline programming workflows. This approach allows us to provide specialist support without the unnecessary complexity that can come with larger delivery structures.

What Makes Us Different

We believe quality starts with understanding—not simply following a specification. Our team works to understand the data, the requirements, and the intended clinical outputs before developing and reviewing programs.

We support clients with:

Clinical Statistical Programming

Tables, listings, figures (TLFs), and analysis datasets.

CDISC Services

SDTM and ADaM development, conversion, and traceability.

PK/PD and NONMEM Support

Fit-for-purpose datasets and validation for pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic analyses.

Independent Validation and QC

Double programming and systematic reviews against specifications and shells.

Programming Support and Training

Practical expertise that helps teams strengthen their capabilities and delivery.
