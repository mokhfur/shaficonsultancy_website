Our Approach

Combining experienced resources, proven processes, and specialised tools, we deliver high-quality, submission-ready outputs. From project initiation to final delivery, our expert team ensures efficiency, consistency, and compliance at every stage.

Resource and Planning

  • Our experts have complete CDISC domain knowledge and experience in different therapeutic areas.
  • Integrated project planner with the emphasis on Risk Management, Managing timelines and maintaining Good Programming Practices (GPP) throughout, ensures the projects run smoothly until completion.
  • Traceability maintained in every aspect of Data Conversion.
  • Staff stay current with the latest CDISC Implementation Guides and regulatory updates.

Tools and Templates

  • In-house developed SAS macros and template programs are utilised to streamline conversion and dataset production.
  • Pinnacle 21 is used to ensure compliance with CDISC validation standards.
  • Standardised tools enable consistency across projects and faster turnaround times.
  • Continuous improvement of tools based on project experience and evolving industry best practices.

Assess and Execute

  • Well-defined SOPs, work instructions, and checklists guide every step.
  • Structured workflows for efficient SDTM and ADaM package generation.
  • Rigorous data checking to maintain integrity and accuracy of deliverables.
  • Integrated validation and verification processes ensure quality and compliance.
