Shafi Chowdhury

Managing Director

Shafi Chowdhury holds MSc. in Statistics and has been programming in SAS since 1993. He has worked for CROs and Pharmaceutical companies across Europe before turning to SAS consultancy. He founded Shafi Consultancy Limited in 2001, and in 2006 he opened the office in Bangladesh.

33+ Years

in the Pharma Industry. Working with top pharma and CROs across international markets.

Knowledge & Thought Leadership

Sharing expertise through international conferences and publications.

Programming & Automation

Developing smarter, metadata-driven approaches to clinical trial reporting.

Over the many years of experience he has seen the same tasks being done in many different ways. This has allowed him to form his opinion on the best approaches and the most efficient process for different tasks in different circumstances. His success over the many years of consultancy means that his opinion is trusted, has credibility and brings results to his clients.

Shafi is a regular presenter at the PSI and PhUSE conferences, believing that it is best when we share our experience and be humble enough to know that we are never too old to learn. He is a member of the PhUSE Transparency working group, PhUSE Good Programming Practice working group, and is the co-chair of the Analytical Risk-Based Monitoring stream of the PhUSE EU Connect conference.
