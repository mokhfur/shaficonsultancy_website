Validation and QC

Independent Validation. Confidence in Every Deliverable.

Ensure the accuracy, consistency, and reliability of your clinical trial programming outputs with Shafi Consultancy’s independent validation and quality control (QC) services.

Our experienced statistical programmers perform independent double programming and systematic reviews of programs, outputs, specifications, and shells to help identify discrepancies before deliverables are finalised. We verify that tables, listings, figures (TLFs), and analysis datasets align with approved specifications and expected results.

Our validation and QC services include:

01

Independent Double Programming

Independently develop programs to verify results and identify potential discrepancies.

02

Program and Output Validation

Review statistical programs and generated outputs against specifications, shells, and expected results.

03

TLF Quality Control

Check tables, listings, and figures for accuracy, consistency, completeness, and presentation requirements.

04

Analysis Dataset QC

Review analysis datasets for consistency with programming specifications and applicable derivation requirements.

05

Discrepancy Reporting

Document identified issues clearly to support investigation, resolution, and revalidation.

With a quality-focused approach and extensive experience in clinical statistical programming, Shafi Consultancy helps pharmaceutical companies and CROs strengthen confidence in their programming deliverables and support reliable clinical trial reporting.
