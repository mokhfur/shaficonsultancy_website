Metalytics Eco-system

24 March 2021, by Shafi Consultancy

“Practice does not make perfect, perfection makes perfect”. One of the most memorable quotes someone once mentioned during a training session almost 25 years ago. Presenting Metalytics V2.04, in pursuit of perfection for a system that efficiently allows us to perform tasks we need to do.

We need to import data, analyse the data and produce SDTM and ADaM datasets, then TLFs, perform validation, documentation, including traceability reports, and finally export everything.

If you think there might be room for improvement of oversight or traceability at your organisation, then get in touch.
