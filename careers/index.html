Learning & Development

Training Programme

At Shafi Consultancy, we take pride in offering a comprehensive training programme designed to build confident, expert statistical programmers.

Over a focused period of at least four months, our trainees gain in-depth knowledge of clinical trial data, data issues that may arise, including techniques to detect and resolve them, programming techniques, and our structured approach to developing consistent, high-quality programs and macros.

This training ensures every programmer understands the Shafi Consultancy way of working, from problem-solving to technical execution.

By the end of the programme, our team members are fully prepared to contribute to live projects with confidence and consistency, helping us deliver efficient and reliable solutions to our clients.
