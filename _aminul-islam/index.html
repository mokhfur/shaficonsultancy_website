Md. Aminul Islam

Team Leader, Statistical Programming

Md. Aminul Islam has over 20 years of experience in clinical trial statistical programming, supporting clinical studies across all phases and a wide range of therapeutic areas, including oncology, respiratory diseases, and diabetes.

Since joining Shafi Consultancy in 2006, Aminul has progressed through several technical and leadership roles and currently leads the Statistical Programming team, overseeing the delivery of clinical trial reporting, CDISC datasets, and PK/PD programming projects.

His expertise spans CDISC SDTM and ADaM standards, Tables, Listings and Figures (TLFs), and PK/PD deliverables, including NONMEM-ready datasets. In his leadership role, he manages project teams, coordinates international client engagements, oversees submission timelines, and provides technical guidance and mentorship to statistical programmers. He also plays an active role in quality and compliance initiatives, promoting consistent programming standards, efficient project delivery, and continuous team development.

Prior to joining Shafi Consultancy, Aminul held programming and team leadership positions across software development and data management environments. He holds an MSc in Statistics from the University of Rajshahi and is SAS Base certified and an Oracle Certified Professional Developer.
