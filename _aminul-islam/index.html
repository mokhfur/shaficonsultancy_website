Aminul Islam

Team Leader

Aminul has an MSc in Statistics from Rajshahi University, as well as being SAS and Oracle certified. He has worked at Shafi Consultancy for 12 years and is the Team Leader for the Clinical Trial Reporting team. Prior to this he worked in a variety of roles at his previous companies, Team Leader, Business Analyst and Programmer.

He has helped to develop and lead a strong team of programmers, reporting trials in many different phases and therapeutic areas, as well as mega trials. Delivering quality results on time is a key strength of his and the team he leads.

Aminul is an expert in the data generation for analyses using NONMEM and WinNonLin. By developing an automated and fully traceable process to create a full summary of the data along with patient profiles, this not only saves time and resources, but allows us to adapt quickly to the many different data structures we receive from clients. He has also used his expertise to help develop training for NONMEM dataset generation.

Aminul has over 5 years of experience in Risk-based Monitoring (RBM), with trials having as many as 16,000 patients, and provides valuable input into improving our own application designed to drive and support this.

During his time at the company he co-wrote a paper on “Managing data issues identified during programming” where he described an automated system to keep track of data issues from identification to resolution. This ensured high quality of data with minimum effort and is just one example of his dedication to process improvement.
